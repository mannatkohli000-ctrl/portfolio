About

Crafting stories with AI

Mannat is an AI video creator focused on brand storytelling, AI UGC-style ads, product advertisements, cinematic AI videos, and short-form social media content.

Alongside video, Mannat also creates graphic design assets such as social media creatives, thumbnails, and promotional visuals so a brand's look stays consistent across every platform.

Working remotely with international brands and creators across the US, UK, UAE, Europe, and Australia, Mannat prioritises clear communication and fast turnaround—delivering polished, platform-ready work that feels intentional and on-brand.
